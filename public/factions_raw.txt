A '''Faction''' is a conditional effect that is found on [[Champion|champions]] of [[Legendary]] [[rarity]] or higher in [[DC: Dark Legion]]. Faction effects activate when a certain number of champions in the same faction are used in a [[squad]] together. When this qualification is met, all champions in the squad that belong to that faction gain a bonus effect for the duration of battle, such as increased damage, increased hit points, or increasing the speed that their energy regenerates.

A Champion can belong to up to four factions: one related to their powers, and up to three that relate to the teams that the Champion is associated with. Multiple faction effects can be active at the same time, and their effects increase in power with the number of champions in the squad that are in that faction.

== Types of Factions ==

=== Power Factions ===
Every non-[[Epic]] champion belongs to one Power Faction. Power Factions represent the type of powers the champion possesses, whether they are based on an energy source, technology or equipment, or some other innate power.

=== Team Factions ===
In addition to their power faction, a Champion may also belong to up to three team factions. These represent the famous groups of [[Hero|heroes]] and/or [[Villain|villains]] that that champion has been associated with. There are currently 16 teams listed in the DC: Dark Legion database. However only 13 of them have a bonus effect, and of those 13, 12 of them have enough Champions belonging to them so that they can potentially reach their maximum bonus.
